About

The person behind the products.

I started as a backend engineer, building production systems at DXC Technology and Quantive. In 2023 I left to build my own products, and found out I could carry the whole thing alone: design, backend, mobile apps, web, infrastructure, and the app store releases at the end.

Gofloo Tech, my DPIIT-recognized startup in Bhopal, is where that happens daily. Floo and Murmur went from idea to live products without a team behind them, which means every decision you see in them, from the database schema to the onboarding copy, passed through one pair of hands.

That is exactly what clients hire: no handoffs, no coordination tax, no "the backend guy says". One person who has shipped before and owns the result.
